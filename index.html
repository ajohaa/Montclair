You're joining New Jersey's second-largest university — nineteen Division III teams, 300+ clubs, and a campus five minutes from NYC by train. Here's what that actually looks like day to day.

Why MSU?

A research university with a small-campus feel — 21,000 students, but classes taught by professors, not TAs.

Sixteen miles from Manhattan, with a free shuttle to the PATH train for internships, shows, or just a Saturday out.

Red Hawk Scholars, First-Year Seminars, and peer mentors mean you're never figuring it out alone.
