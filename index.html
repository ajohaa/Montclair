Why MSU?

#1

Public University in New Jersey

Ranked the No. 1 public institution in New Jersey in the Wall Street Journal/College Pulse 2026 Best Colleges in the U.S. list.

Top 30

Public in the Nation

Ranked among the top 30 public institutions nationwide, underscoring the strength of our academics and outcomes.

300+

Academic Programs

At Montclair, you’ll have access to more than 300 academic majors, minors, concentrations and certificate programs.

12 Miles

In the Big Apple’s Backyard

The Montclair campus sits just 12 miles from Manhattan in the towns of Montclair, Clifton and Little Falls, New Jersey. Our Bloomfield campus is 14 miles from Midtown, giving you two areas with mass transit options that will take you to the heart of the city.

252-Acre

Big Campus, Small Feel

Montclair’s 252-acre suburban campus gives you the resources of a large research university with a sense of belonging normally found at smaller schools. Our Bloomfield campus provides an even more intimate setting, giving you the best of both worlds.

5

Top Graduate Programs

Five of Montclair’s graduate programs in business, Education, Public Health, Audiology and Clinical Psychology were ranked by U.S. News & World Report in its 2026 Best Graduate Schools rankings.

17-1

Small Class Sizes

Our 17-1 student-to-teacher ratio makes the average class size at Montclair just 24 students. Your professors will know your name, and will push you to be your best while supporting you every step of the way.

23

NCAA Division III and USCAA Athletics

Our Montclair campus features 19 NCAA Division III Red Hawks athletics teams, while our Bloomfield campus features four USCAA intercollegiate athletics programs - giving you the chance to watch some of the top teams in the country throughout the year.

Top 10

Best for Social Mobility in Nation

Ranked No. 9 nationally by U.S. News & World Report for advancing students from all backgrounds. Nearly half of Montclair undergraduates are Pell Grant recipients, reflecting a deep commitment to access and student success.
