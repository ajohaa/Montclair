Admissions & Aid

Admissions Process

Applications open August 1 and are reviewed on a rolling basis, so applying early gives you the best shot at your preferred housing and scholarships.

You'll need your high school transcript, a personal essay, and one letter of recommendation. Standardized test scores are optional and won't count against you if you leave them out.

Most freshmen hear back within four to six weeks. Admitted students get access to a portal for choosing housing, registering for orientation, and connecting with a peer mentor.

Transfer and international students follow a slightly different checklist — your admissions counselor will confirm exactly what's needed once your application is started.

Financial Aid Info

File the FAFSA as soon as it opens in the fall — Montclair uses it to determine both need-based aid and most merit scholarships automatically.

Roughly 80% of MSU freshmen receive some form of financial aid, whether that's grants, work-study, loans, or a scholarship tied to your major or GPA.

Your award letter arrives alongside your admission decision, so you'll know your real cost before you have to commit to a deposit.

For more information, please contact the Office of Admissions at admissions@montclair.edu or (973) 655-4200 — we're happy to walk through your specific situation.
